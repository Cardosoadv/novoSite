## 2025-05-14 - Skip Link and ARIA Feedback
**Learning:** In fixed-navbar layouts, a "Skip to Content" link must be carefully positioned and given a higher z-index to be visible over the glassmorphism header. Additionally, descriptive labels on generic "Read More" links significantly improve screen reader navigation by providing context.
**Action:** Always include a skip link for layouts with more than 5 navigation items and use context-rich ARIA labels for repetitive action links.
