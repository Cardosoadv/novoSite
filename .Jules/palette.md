## 2025-05-15 - Improving Keyboard Accessibility with Skip Links and Focus Styles
**Learning:** For a single-page application (SPA) with a fixed or large navigation header, keyboard users must tab through every menu item on every page load. Providing a "Skip to Content" link is a critical accessibility (a11y) pattern that significantly improves the experience for screen reader and keyboard-only users. Additionally, relying on default browser focus rings can be inconsistent; explicitly styling `:focus-visible` with the brand's accent color (e.g., `--gold`) ensures high visibility and maintains design consistency.
**Action:** Always include a `skip-link` at the very beginning of `App.vue` that targets the `<main>` content area. Ensure the target has `tabindex="-1"` to allow programmatic focus without making it a tab stop. Use `focus-visible` to style focus rings for better accessibility without affecting mouse users.
